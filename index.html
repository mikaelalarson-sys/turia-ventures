What I help with

Services

Spanish bureaucracy can be confusing and time-consuming, especially in a new language. I will assist you through each step including submitting requests and attending appointments.

Visa Guidance Help navigating the main visa options for moving to Spain (Non-Lucrative, Digital Nomad, and others), including understanding requirements, preparing documents, and knowing what to expect at each stage.
NIE & TIE Guidance on obtaining your NIE (Spanish tax ID) and, for non-EU citizens, your TIE residency card. Required early and needed for almost everything else.
Apartment & Neighborhood Guidance Find the right area and apartment, schedule viewings and sign a contract.
Utility & Cell Phone Set Up Get set up with a Spanish SIM card, home internet, electricity, and gas. Guidance on navigating providers, contracts, and account setup.
Padron (Empadron) Officially register your address with the municipality. Required for school enrollment, healthcare access, and many other local services.
School Enrollment School applications for public and concertado (semi-private) schools. Includes requests to recognize foreign academic credit for middle and high school students.
Extracurricular Activities Find the right sport, music, art, or language class, arrange trial sessions, and handle enrollment paperwork.
Digital Signature Set up the digital certificate required to sign documents and access most Spanish government portals online, without visiting an office in person.
Large Family Card (Familia Numerosa) For families with three or more children. The card provides priority points for school enrollment and discounts on transport, museums, public fees, and more.
Spanish Driver's License (Carnet) Full guidance on each step in order to obtain your driver's license, what you can complete independently, and what requires a driving school.
Buying a Car Walk through buying a used car in Spain, from searching listings and negotiating, to DGT paperwork and completing the transfer of ownership.

About

Hi, my name is Mikaela. I moved to Valencia with my husband and three children over seven years ago. I specialize in helping families complete the necessary processes to move to Valencia, get settled, enroll kids in school, and handle the other steps along the way, so you can enjoy this new experience and feel at home.

When we arrived, our kids (3, 7, and 9) entered the local public school in our neighborhood. It was a great way to integrate, make friends, and learn Spanish. We worked with a relocation assistant to help us navigate the process, and it was an investment that really helped us launch successfully.

I'd love to help others who need assistance with various processes as they move to Valencia. I can answer general questions and help you complete each step along the way. Spanish bureaucracy is confusing and time-consuming, especially when you're new and doing it in a second language. I'll help you navigate it with confidence and save you time, money, and stress. I'll guide you starting with the most critical items needed right away as well as other items that come in handy later on such as obtaining a Spanish driver's license, a large family card or the digital signature which is used for many government processes.

Our original plan to spend a one year sabbatical in Spain transformed into choosing to settle here long term and falling in love with Valencia. Feel free to set up an initial consultation and I'll create a custom plan to help you with your specific needs.

Mikaela
