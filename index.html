About Mikaela

Seven years in Valencia, and counting

Hi, my name is Mikaela. I moved to Valencia with my husband and three children over seven years ago. I specialize in helping families complete the necessary processes to move to Valencia, get settled, enroll kids in school, and handle the other steps along the way, so you can enjoy this new experience and feel at home.

When we arrived, our kids (3, 7, and 9) entered the local public school in our neighborhood. It was a great way to integrate, make friends, and learn Spanish. We worked with a relocation assistant to help us navigate the process, and it was an investment that really helped us launch successfully.

I'd love to help others who need assistance with various processes as they move to Valencia. I can answer general questions and help you complete each step along the way. As a newcomer, unfamiliar processes can be confusing and overwhelming and I will help you navigate them with confidence, save you time, money and reduce stress. I'll guide you starting with the most critical items needed right away as well as other items that come in handy later on such as obtaining a Spanish drivers license (carnet,) a large family card (familia numerosa) or the digital signature which is used for many government processes.

Our original plan to spend a one year sabbatical in Spain transformed into choosing to settle here long term and falling in love with Valencia. Feel free to set up an initial consultation and I'll create a custom plan to help you with your specific needs.

Mikaela

What I help with

Services

As a newcomer, unfamiliar processes can be confusing and overwhelming. I will assist you through each step including submitting requests and attending appointments.

01 Apartment & Neighborhood Guidance Find the right area and apartment, schedule viewings and sign a contract.
02 Empadron Officially register your address.
03 School Enrollment School applications in public and semi-public schools.
04 Schooling Recognition For middle and high school students to get recognition for foreign academic credit.
05 Spanish Driver's License Guide to complete the written and practical driving test to obtain the carnet.
06 Familia Numerosa Large family card for additional points to enter into schools as well as discount on transport, museums, and more.
07 Digital Signature Set up digital signature needed for most online government processes.
08 Utility & Cell Phone Set Up Assistance setting up a cellular service, wifi, electricity, gas, etc.
09 Extracurricular Activities Finding activities, setting up trials, and enrollment.
10 Buying a Car Walk through of the steps to buy a used car.
